Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how VEXA Technologies, the working name of a private project run by Dev, an independent freelance web developer in Dublin, Ireland ("we", "the owner"), accesses, uses, stores and protects data. VEXA Technologies is not a registered company. The project is used only by its owner, through the owner's own Google account, and is not offered to the public.

Limited Use. VEXA Technologies' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Google data the project accesses

The project connects to the owner's Google account with the following permissions, and uses each one only as described.

Permission (scope)What it is used for
Google Sheets
spreadsheets, drive.file, drive.metadata		 Reading and updating the owner's private lead-management spreadsheet: business names, categories, locations, public websites and public business contact details, research notes, draft text and status. The Drive permissions are requested by the standard Google Sheets connection of the automation software the project uses; the project uses them only to work with its own spreadsheet and does not browse, index or copy other files.
Gmail (read-only)
gmail.readonly		 Checking for replies in the specific email threads the owner started with businesses, identified by thread IDs saved in the spreadsheet, so that replies, bounces and opt-out requests are noticed and respected. It does not search or browse the rest of the mailbox. Only the reply category, the time of the reply and message identifiers are recorded in the spreadsheet; message contents are not stored there. Replies are classified with simple rules, not with AI.
Gmail (compose)
gmail.compose		 Creating email drafts in the owner's Gmail Drafts folder for the owner to review. Google defines this permission as allowing an app to manage drafts and send emails. The project uses it only to create drafts: it contains no step that sends email, and every email is reviewed and sent manually by the owner from Gmail.

2. How the data is used

  • Google user data is used only to provide the project's own features to its owner: managing leads, preparing drafts and tracking replies.
  • It is not sold, rented or shared for advertising, and is not used for advertising, credit or lending decisions.
  • No one other than the owner reads it, except where required to comply with law or to investigate abuse or security issues.
  • Google Workspace data obtained through Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

AI-assisted drafting

When the owner explicitly starts a drafting run, limited lead information from the spreadsheet (business name, category, location and short notes about the business's public website) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to help write a draft. Gmail message content is never sent to any AI service. Drafts are always reviewed by the owner before anything is sent.

Business information

Leads consist of publicly available business information, obtained from Google Maps Platform and from the businesses' own public websites. Businesses are contacted only through publicly listed business contact details, every message offers a clear way to opt out, and businesses that opt out are not contacted again.

3. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • The project runs on a private server in the European Union (Helsinki, Finland), hosted by Hetzner Online.
  • The server is reachable only over an encrypted private network used by the owner's own devices; the project's tools are not exposed to the public internet.
  • Google access tokens are stored encrypted by the automation software and are never shared.
  • A read-only copy of the spreadsheet is kept on the same private server for the owner's dashboard, and is replaced each time it is refreshed.
  • The project's own backups are encrypted before they are stored, and encrypted copies are kept on the owner's own computer. The hosting provider also keeps short-term system backups of the server.
  • The owner's own computer also holds a private development copy of the project.
  • Access is limited to the owner.

5. Service providers

  • Google: Google Sheets, Gmail and Google Maps Platform APIs.
  • Anthropic: Claude API, used only for drafting as described above.
  • Hetzner Online: server hosting in Finland (EU).
  • Tailscale: encrypted private networking between the owner's devices and the server.
  • GitHub: hosts this public website only. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

  • The owner can revoke the project's access to their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the project can no longer read the spreadsheet, read email or create drafts.
  • Any business or person can ask for their information to be deleted, or opt out of contact, by replying to an email they received or by writing to devprashath5@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days. Data is removed from the spreadsheet promptly and disappears from encrypted backups as they expire under the retention periods above. If you have opted out, a minimal record is kept only so the opt-out can be honoured.

7. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data about you, and you may object to its processing. The owner relies on legitimate interests to contact businesses using publicly available business contact details. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at www.dataprotection.ie.

8. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, tracking or third-party scripts.
