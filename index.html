VEXA Technologies is the working name of a private business-automation and lead-management project run by Dev, an independent freelance web developer based in Dublin, Ireland. It is not a registered company and it is not a service offered to the public.

What the project does

The project helps one person run a small, careful outreach process for web-design work with local businesses. It is used only by its owner, through the owner's own Google account.

Find

Identifies local businesses from publicly listed business information.

Research

Reviews each business's public website presence and records short notes in a private spreadsheet.

Draft

Prepares email drafts in the owner's Gmail Drafts folder. Nothing is sent automatically.

Follow up

Checks replies to emails the owner sent, so opt-out requests are respected and recorded.

How it works responsibly

  • A person approves everything. Every email is reviewed and sent manually by the owner. The project cannot send email on its own initiative.
  • Opt-outs are honoured. Every message offers a clear way to opt out, and opted-out businesses are never contacted again.
  • Private by design. The system runs on a private server in the European Union and is not reachable from the public internet.
  • Minimal data. Only publicly available business information and the owner's own working notes are kept.
